Long forms & wizards

Use a wizard when the later steps depend on earlier answers, or when someone needs to stop halfway and come back. Otherwise use one long page with clear sections. It's quicker to fill in and easier to check.

Wizard: add a supplier

Four steps, each checked before you can continue. Errors are listed at the top of the step and marked on each field. Back never loses what you typed. Try clicking Continue with the fields empty.

Rules for long forms

  • One column. Put fields side by side only when they belong together, like sort code and account number.
  • Mark the exception. If most fields are required, mark the optional ones, and the other way round.
  • Check on submit, then as people fix things. Put a summary at the top that moves focus to the first problem.
  • Never clear a form because of an error. Keep every value the user typed, including the wrong ones.
  • Save drafts for anything that takes longer than two minutes, and say that you're doing it.
