Depth & motion

Shadows show that something is floating. Motion shows where something came from or went. If an effect does neither, leave it out.

Three lifts

The shadows are warm brown, not grey, so they look like paper on a desk. In dark mode they get darker and borders take over most of the separation.

Flat
Panels, tables
--lift-1
Buttons, inputs
--lift-2
Menus, hovered cards
--lift-3
Dialogs, toasts

Three speeds, two curves

Entering animations ease out: they start fast and settle. Exits ease in and run quicker, because once someone has dismissed something they don't need to watch it leave. Nothing takes longer than 260ms.

--dur-1 90ms
Hover, press, colour
--dur-2 160ms
Menus, switches, tabs
--dur-3 260ms
Dialogs, drawers, toasts

Reduced motion

When someone has asked their system for reduced motion, every duration token drops to zero. Things still change state, they just don't move. Skeleton shimmer stops too. You don't have to do anything per component: it's all handled in tokens.css.

@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce) {
  :root { --dur-1: 0ms; --dur-2: 0ms; --dur-3: 0ms; }
}
