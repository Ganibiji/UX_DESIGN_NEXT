Accordion

Built on <details>, so it works with no script and the browser's find-in-page can open it. Good for FAQs and settings people rarely change. Bad for anything people need to compare.

When does a period lock?
At midnight on the last working day of the following month, unless a controller locks it earlier. You'll get a reminder three days before.
Can I reverse a posted journal?
Yes, until the period locks. Reversing creates a mirror journal dated the same day, so the audit trail keeps both.
Who can see payroll figures?
Only members of the Payroll team and the two named directors. Everyone else sees payroll as a single line in reports.
