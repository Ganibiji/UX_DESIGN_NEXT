Approvals

An approval is a decision someone will be asked about in an audit. Show everything needed to decide on one screen, make the decision explicit, and record who decided, when and why.

Purchase order approval

Try each decision. Sending a request back needs a reason, because "returned" with no explanation is a dead end. Every decision is added to the history and can be undone for a short time.

Rules

  • Put the amount on the approve button. "Approve £3,920" is harder to click by mistake than "Approve".
  • Show what's unusual before the buttons. Overspends, first payments to new bank details and duplicates belong in a callout above the decision, not in a tooltip.
  • Sending back needs a reason. Approving doesn't. Don't make the common case slower.
  • Never let someone approve their own request. Hide the decision panel and explain why.
  • The history is permanent. Undo adds a new entry. It doesn't delete the old one.
