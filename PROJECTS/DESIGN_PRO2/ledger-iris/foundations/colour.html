White, lavender, and one indigo

A clean white workspace, a lavender index and soft pastel accents. One deep indigo carries the primary action and marks what's selected or in focus, so the eye always knows where to go next.

Surfaces

White for lists and forms, apricot cream for the record you're reading, lavender for navigation and selection, and a lilac-to-apricot wash behind every page title. Depth comes from these tones first. Shadows are only for things that float.

Dark theme

Dark mode is a deep ink-violet, not pure black. Every token keeps its name and gets a value designed for the dark surface; nothing is inverted automatically. Indigo becomes lighter so it still reads, and text on the primary button turns dark.
