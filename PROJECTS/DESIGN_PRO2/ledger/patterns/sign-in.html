Sign-in

The first screen everyone sees, and the one they least want to see. Keep it short, let password managers do their job, and when something goes wrong, help people get in.

What goes wrong, and what we say

SituationMessage
Wrong password"That email and password don't match." Don't say which one is wrong; that tells an attacker which accounts exist.
Third failurePause for five minutes and offer a reset. Say how long the pause lasts.
SSO-only account"Northwind signs in through Okta." Then show a single button that goes there.
Session expired"You were signed out after 30 minutes of inactivity." Return people to the page they were on, with their unsaved work kept where possible.

Rules

  • Use the right autocomplete tokens: username and current-password. Password managers depend on them.
  • Allow pasting. Blocking paste stops people using password managers and makes passwords weaker.
  • Offer "Show password" instead of a confirmation field.
  • Don't clear the email after a failure. Clear the password and put focus back on it.
