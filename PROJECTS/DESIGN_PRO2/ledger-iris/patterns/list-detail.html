List & detail

Most back-office work is "go through the pile". The list stays put, the record opens beside it, and the arrow keys move to the next one without reaching for the mouse.

Rules

  • Keep the list where it is. Acting on a record must not scroll the list or reset its filters.
  • After an action, move to the next item. Show a short toast with Undo, and don't make people close the record first.
  • Deep-link every record. The URL changes when the selection does, so people can share "this one".
  • On phones, show one pane at a time. The detail opens as its own page with a back link to the list.
