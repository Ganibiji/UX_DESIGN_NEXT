Editors

For writing more than a field can hold: policy notes, approval comments, integration config. Keep the toolbar short. Most people only use bold and lists.

Rich text

Formatting buttons sit in groups, and each one shows whether it's active at the cursor. Every button has a keyboard shortcut. The word count sits at the bottom, out of the way.

Travel & subsistence

Book the cheapest reasonable option. Reasonable means you arrive in time and rested. It doesn't mean the 05:40 train.

  • Hotels: up to £140 a night in London, £95 elsewhere.
  • Meals: actual cost, with receipts, up to £35 a day.
If you're unsure, ask before you book. It's always fine to ask.
