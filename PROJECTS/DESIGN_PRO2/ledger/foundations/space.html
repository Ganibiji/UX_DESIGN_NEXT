Space & layout

Everything is spaced in multiples of four. The rule that matters more: things that belong together sit closer to each other than to anything else.

Spacing scale

Nine steps. Inside components you'll mostly see 4, 8 and 12. Between components, 16 and 24. Between sections, 48 and up.

Proximity does the grouping

Before you add a border or a box, try adding space. A label sits 6px from its field, and fields sit 16px apart. Groups of fields sit 32px apart and get a heading.

Grid

Twelve columns with 24px gutters on desktop. On tablets it drops to eight columns, and on phones to four columns with 16px margins. Most app screens only need two layouts, shown below.

Corners

Small radii. Ledger is meant to look drawn with a pen, not inflated. Larger things get slightly rounder corners so the curve looks the same at every size.
