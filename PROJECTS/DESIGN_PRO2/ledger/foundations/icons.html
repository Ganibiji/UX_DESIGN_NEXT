Icons

Drawn for this system: a 24px grid, 1.6px stroke, round ends, no fills. They're meant to look like they were drawn with the same pen as the type.

Construction

Keep a 2px margin inside the 24px box. Keep corners at 1.5px radius and avoid tiny details that disappear at 16px. If an icon needs a caption to make sense, it probably shouldn't be an icon.

  1. 24 × 24 box, drawn at 1×. Shown at 18px by default, 15px small, 22px large.
  2. 2px live-area margin. The dashed green line.
  3. 1.6px stroke, round caps and joins. Scaling is fine; changing the stroke width isn't.
  4. currentColor, so icons always take on the colour of their text.

Usage
