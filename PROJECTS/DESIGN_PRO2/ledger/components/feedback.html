Feedback

How the system talks back. Match the volume of the message to how much it matters — most things deserve a whisper.

Sits in the page, next to what it's about. Each tone has its own icon and a left rule, so the meaning survives without colour.

Full width, top of the app, about the whole system: maintenance, trial ending, a sandbox you're working in. One at a time.

Toast

Confirms something that just happened, in the corner, then leaves. If the action can be undone, the toast offers it and stays longer. Hovering pauses the timer. Never put an error that needs fixing in a toast.

// From script
ledgerToast({ title: 'Journal posted', text: 'All 12 entries balance.', kind: 'pos', action: 'Undo' });

If you know how far along it is, show a bar and the number. If you don't, an indeterminate bar or spinner — plus words about what's happening.

Skeleton

Shape of the content before the content. Match the real layout closely, or the page jumps when data arrives — which is worse than a spinner.

Empty states

Three different situations that look the same but need different words: nothing yet, nothing matches, and nothing you're allowed to see.
