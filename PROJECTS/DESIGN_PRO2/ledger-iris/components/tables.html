Tables

The workhorse. People scan down columns far more than across rows, so we design for the column: aligned, tabular, and with nothing in the way.

Five rules

  • Numbers right, text left. Decimal points line up, so magnitudes can be compared without reading.
  • Tabular figures always. font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums is on every .num cell.
  • Rules, not stripes. Hairlines between rows; hover tints the row you're on. Zebra stripes fight with selection colour.
  • Headers stick. Once you've scrolled 40 rows, "which column was this?" is the only question left.
  • Units in the header, not the cell. "Amount (GBP)" once beats "£" fifty times — unless currencies are mixed.

Density

Three row heights: 32px dense for grids and power users, 42px default, 54px relaxed for rows with two lines. Let people choose; remember the choice.
