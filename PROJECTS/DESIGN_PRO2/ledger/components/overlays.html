Menus & overlays

Things that float. The rule of thumb: the more an overlay blocks, the more it has to earn its place.

From light to heavy

OverlayBlocks the page?Use for
TooltipNoNaming an icon. A few words, never anything you can click.
PopoverNoA small amount of extra detail or a quick edit, close to its trigger.
MenuNoA list of actions. Closes after one is chosen.

Popover & tooltip

A popover can hold a sentence, a link or a tiny form. A tooltip holds a few words and appears after a short delay, so it doesn't flicker as the pointer passes.

Dialog

Built on the native <dialog>, which traps focus and restores it for free. The title is a question or an outcome; the buttons answer it.

We'll check these details with the bank before the first payment goes out.

Post 12 entries to October?

The entries total £48,210.55 and balance. Once posted they appear in reports straight away. You can reverse them until the period locks on 31 October.

Delete Northwind IE?

This deletes the entity, its 2,318 transactions and its bank feeds. Reports for the group will change. This can't be undone.
