White, mint, and one green

A clean white workspace, a mint index and soft pastel accents. One deep green carries the primary action and marks what's selected or in focus, so the eye always knows where to go next.

Surfaces

White for lists and forms, warm cream for the record you're reading, mint for navigation and selection, and a pale wash behind every page title. Depth comes from these tones first. Shadows are only for things that float.

Dark theme

Dark mode is a deep forest green, not pure black. Every token keeps its name and gets a value designed for the dark surface; nothing is inverted automatically. Green becomes lighter so it still reads, and text on the primary button turns dark.
