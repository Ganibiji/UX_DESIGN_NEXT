Pickers

Dates, times, files and colours. Each picker is a helper for a field people can also type into — never the only way in.

Date picker

The field accepts typed dates in any sensible format. The calendar is for when someone needs to see the week — "the Friday after next".

Banks don't process on weekends.

Date range

Two clicks: start, then end. Presets beat calendars for reporting — most people want "last month", not 1 September to 30 September.
